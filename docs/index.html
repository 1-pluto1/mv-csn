Interactive Experimental Results (Verified Multi-Seed Data)

All numbers loaded directly from results/*.json (seeds: 42, 1024, 2025)
Takeaway: Trained strictly on clean SMS and tested on 3,395 unseen real adversarial variants, standalone MV-CSN v2 (1.37M) jumps to F1 = 0.9951 (+3.83% over RBT3, +4.09% over LZASD, McNemar p < 1e-6), and RBT3 + MV-CSN v2 sets the overall peak at 0.9964 (GLYPH = 0.9827, SPLIT = 0.9551).
Do we need a large LLM? No. Zero-Shot Qwen3-4B-Instruct suffers 59.5% false positives on clean SMS (HamAcc = 0.4050, F1 = 0.8671, 30.74 ms) and 4-Shot ICL only reaches 0.9421 at 60.98 ms. Even after 3-seed Clean-LoRA fine-tuning, Qwen3-0.6B merely ties MV-CSN v2 (0.9951 vs. 0.9951, McNemar p = 0.3833) at 438× more parameters and 36.5× slower speed. If LLM review is desired, an uncertainty-gated Cascade routes only 0.71% (24/3,395) of ambiguous cases to Qwen3-4B for 0.598 ms/SMS average latency.
Takeaway: Removing the Split/Noise Preprocessor causes the sharpest degradation (−0.0253 F1, 12× higher variance), followed by RoCBert Graph Consistency (−0.0026), Context Gate (−0.0008), and Dynamic OOV Bridge (−0.0007).
Dataset Bias Discovery: In Course Benchmark A, Ham posts are long BBS messages (median 191 chars) vs. short Spam SMS (median 53 chars), allowing TFIDF-SVM to exploit length shortcuts (0.9991). Our debiased Benchmark B (SMS-Matched 16.5K) matches length histograms (p50 = 51 vs. 53) with zero template-cluster leakage.
Failure-Mode Audit: Out of 3,395 unseen real-variant SMS, Seed-42 MV-CSN v2 makes only 20 errors (0.59%) — 8 FP (all bank-card/phone/futures messages) and 12 FN (including 4 upstream corpus label-noise notices such as weather warnings, COVID health advisories, and a takeout food order; excluding those 4 yields 99.65% true spam recall). Only 0.74% (25/3,395) of test SMS fall in the (0.05, 0.95) band, yet they capture 35.0% (7/20) of all errors (47× enrichment).
